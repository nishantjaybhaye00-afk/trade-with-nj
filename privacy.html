Privacy Policy

Draft template — review and complete before launch.

This page should explain what information you collect, why you collect it, how it is used, how long it is kept, and how customers can contact you about their data.

Information collected

Describe information collected through checkout, customer support, and delivery, such as contact details and transaction references. Do not publish information you do not actually collect.

Payments

Explain which payment provider processes payments and link to its privacy policy. Do not store card or UPI credentials yourself.

Sharing and retention

Describe any service providers who receive customer information, how long records are retained, and how users can request access or deletion where applicable.

Contact

Add a monitored business email address and an appropriate contact process before publishing.

This is a starter template, not legal advice. Adapt it to your actual practices and applicable law before accepting customers.
