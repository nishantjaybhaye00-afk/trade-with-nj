# Trade With NJ — Premium website

A responsive, dark editorial-style landing page for a trading-education PDF.

## Files
- `index.html` — main landing page
- `privacy.html` — privacy policy starter template
- `terms.html` — terms starter template

## Publish with GitHub Pages
1. Upload these files to the root of your `trade-with-nj` repository.
2. Open **Settings → Pages**.
3. Select **Deploy from a branch**, branch `main`, folder `/(root)`.
4. Wait for the green deployment check and open your Pages URL.

## Before selling
- Replace draft chapter descriptions with the exact contents of your finished PDF.
- Configure a payment provider and replace the placeholder purchase button with your real checkout link.
- Configure and test secure delivery after verified payment.
- Complete privacy, terms, refund policy and business contact details.
- Never place payment secret keys or your paid PDF in this public repository.

The page intentionally displays that checkout is not connected. Do not accept payments until the product and delivery flow are ready.
